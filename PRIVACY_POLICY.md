# TrailReady privacy policy

Effective date: 10/22/2026
Developer: Muhammad Amjad · Contact: amjad.venv@gmail.com

TrailReady stores everything on your device. It has no account system and does not request the
INTERNET permission, so it cannot send your data anywhere.

## What the app stores (on your device only)
Checklists, equipment, trips, emergency contacts (names, phone numbers, notes), your emergency notes,
and settings (theme, haptics, reminder choice).

## What we collect
Nothing. No analytics, advertising, crash-reporting or tracking SDKs. No data is shared with third parties.

## Permissions
- **Notifications** (only if you turn on departure reminders): to show a reminder on the day before a trip.
- **Alarm scheduling / boot**: to restore your reminders after the phone restarts.
- No location, camera, microphone, contacts or storage permission. Backup uses the system file picker.

## Phone calls
The app opens your dialer with a number filled in. You must press call yourself.

## Backups
"Export backup" creates a JSON file in a place you choose. It is not encrypted and includes personal
details. Store and share it carefully. Importing a backup only reads the file you select.

## Deleting your data
Use Settings → delete all data, or uninstall the app. Because nothing is sent off-device, there is nothing
for us to delete on a server.

## Children
The app does not knowingly collect any information from anyone.

## Changes
If this policy changes, the new version will be published with the app update.

## Safety notice
TrailReady is not a substitute for professional medical advice or emergency services. In an emergency call 911.
